# The Big Ones

## New Employee - The Mycologist

A member of the first expedition to New Atlas after the fungal outbreak, the Mycologist has been trapped planet-side (moon-side??) for over a year. This has made her… a little strange.

We’ve been working on the Mycologist for quite a while and we think you’ll have a lot of fun with her set of upgrades and her abilities: **Sporelift** and **Fungal Skewer**. Plus, a passive **infection** ability… But first, you’ll have to rescue her in **Expedition Alpha.**

![The Mycologist]({{ '/mycopunk/img/Mycologist.webp' | relative_url }}){: style="width: 500px; margin: 24px auto"}

## Mission - Population Control

A new normal mission! Clear out areas of high enemy density, and maybe tussle with a couple Abominations.

![Population Control]({{ '/mycopunk/img/Abomination.webp' | relative_url }})

## Mini Special Assignment - The Couch

Help Roachard find the perfect fit and rescue a couch for the HUB.

Also you now can sit in some chairs.

![The Diffraction Driver]({{ '/mycopunk/img/Couch.webp' | relative_url }})

## Weapon - The Diffraction Driver

An ion scattergun that fires in a short-range cone. You can spin up its halo to make the cone smaller and longer. This weapon can be unlocked with some resources after you reach level 10.

![The Diffraction Driver]({{ '/mycopunk/img/DiffractionDriver.webp' | relative_url }})

## Snowball Grenade

A cryo grenade! The Snowball is unlocked from X11-B.

![Snowball Grenade]({{ '/mycopunk/img/Snowball.webp' | relative_url }})

## X11-B

A new region on New Atlas’ moonlet. X11-B’s difficult missions will test your best loadouts.

![X11-B]({{ '/mycopunk/img/X11B.webp' | relative_url }})

## Hats

You can wear hats. On your head. Most hats come from the new Fulfillments!

![Hat]({{ '/mycopunk/img/Hat.webp' | relative_url }})

## Fulfillments

An in-game achievement system for tracking your mastery and watching a big number go up. Many Fulfillments reward cosmetics.

Some Fulfillments are retroactive and some aren’t, depending on which stats we were already tracking.

![Fulfillments]({{ '/mycopunk/img/Fulfillments.webp' | relative_url }})

## Sit Emotes

Each employee has a default sit emote! Time to plant yer ass and watch your friends do the mission.

![Sitting]({{ '/mycopunk/img/Sit.webp' | relative_url }})

## HUB Rework

It’s cool. Just check it out for yourself.

## Infusion Effectiveness

Upgrade effectiveness is affected by how much its pattern size has changed. Infusing a smaller pattern into an upgrade will reduce the effectiveness of most of its stats, while infusing a larger pattern will improve its stats!
