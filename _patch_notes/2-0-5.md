## Racetrack

- The racetrack is BACK IN THE HUB!

## Crashing

- [PS5] Fixed an issue where the game would freeze if you clicked a lobby button twice in quick succession

## Upgrades

- (Diffraction Driver) Swapped min/max stats of Full Potential when boosting
- (Au-Si Jackrabbit) Added a mild stacking penalty to Heavy in Ouroboros
- (Accelerator) Fixed Shake The Nest not regenerating ammo if you switched to another weapon mid-burst
