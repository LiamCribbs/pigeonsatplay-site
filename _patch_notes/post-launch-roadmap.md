HI MYCOPUNKERS!! We've been thrilled to see so many people jump in for Mycopunk's full release! But we aren't done yet, and it's finally time to share our roadmap for post-1.0 updates.

![Roadmap]({{ '/mycopunk/img/PostLaunchRoadmapS.png' | relative_url }}){: margin: 24px auto"}

We have 3 **FREE** major updates planned throughout the next year! The first update, **NEMESIS**, arrives in early 2027 and features a **new weapon** and a **dungeon**.

The Nemesis weapon is still in its early stages, but as you might have guessed from the roadmap, it's a **melee blade**! We're excited for you to get your hands on it (literally).

For Nemesis, we wanted to try a new kind of mission, which you'll be able to experience in its dungeon. It'll be a more linear level with a Big Bad Boss at the end.

The next two updates feature ??? and ???, which is to say we don't know yet. We have some ideas floating around, but if there's something YOU want to see added, you should tell us and help shape our plans for update 2 and 3.

Thank you all so much for playing our favorite game :)
