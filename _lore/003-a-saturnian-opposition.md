# A Saturnian Opposition

`Secure Email from Chief Science Officer Gale Kistone [DECEASED] to Minister of Saturnian Defense Pim Hackwall`

Hey Hacks, sorry for springing this on you out of the blue, but I haven’t been able to let go of that research base we lost in the ‘terrorism’ scuffle a couple years ago. Saxon’s got an itchy trigger finger sure, but it’s still super suspicious, even for them. Adding the hole into the mix just boils it over for me.

Well so first of all, we never figured out how the hole got there in the first place. But I think the wackos might have been right to call it a weapons test. I still don’t believe it went through the *entire* moon, but I think it’s worth sending a stealth team down there to re-examine it now that the radiation’s mostly blown over.

Main reason I say anything is because of that new gravity drive bullshit the Saxies are peddling out. They got the whole Otherspace thing figured out for it, but I think they aren’t telling the whole story. I mean: a ship-sized hole appears in a moon, the station that’s looking into it gets vaporized because of a Saxon ‘targeting error,’ then they unveil this thing that goes shit-your-pants speed a couple years later?

I mean come *ON*. They *HAD* to have tried making it work outside of an Otherspace first. I would’ve given it a shot, we’ve been chasing after full FTL since before I was born. It’s the only conceivable way that hole could’ve popped up, I’d stake my life on it.

How’d they fuck it up? No clue. My guess is that getting past light-speed in this dimension comes with a couple snags, and their flight computers couldn’t take it.

They’re so cagey about how the stuff works though that it’s hard to tell whether they’re actually flying *through* their Otherspace, or if they’re just folding it like we do with Slipspace. If they are actually chugging through that bitch, then I feel like we have a pretty clear-cut case for getting their exclusivity rights tossed over this. There’s no way it would hold up if word gets out that they can launch missiles and shit at LIGHT SPEED for God’s sake. Right?

I’ll attach some scribbles that go over the math based on what we know about the hole. It’s more than enough for me to want to do something, but I get that it’d be a risk.

-- Gale
