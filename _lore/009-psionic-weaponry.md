# Psionic Weaponry

`Report From Geman Fitzgerald Jr. - Head of Saxon Weapons Development`

Psionic weapons are relatively useless outside of Hashetty hands, and I only say ‘relatively’ because you can still get a few good uses out of them before they run out of charge. The assumption is that Hashetty soldiers are trained in some kind of basic Psionic technique that allows them to sustain their weapons with their own ‘willpower’, or something.

Given that we have not yet figured out the trick behind Psionics, I have taken steps to partner with the local robotics division to see about building an artificial sentience modeled after Hashetty brain-scans to see if that might give us the spark we need. Our own ability to use Psionic weapons and technology *at all* likely rules out their biology being the key, so the next natural step is to try and replicate the way they *think*, if nothing else.

The interesting, and rather troubling thing is that we have recovered a handful of Psionic weapons that do not appear to be running out of charge. They are as follows: a miniaturized version of one of their Dancer Javelins, a cup that can hold roughly two tonnes of liquid, a cannon that fires out lava (we do not know where the lava comes from), and a copper ball that is only as heavy as one expects it to be (I cannot explain it any more than that). Testing with these is rather limited however, as while their charge appears to be limitless, the same has been thought about many relics that now lay drained, broken, or ‘cursed’, for lack of a better word.

Intelligence suggests that the Federation has made a similar lack of progress, while possessing (thankfully) a similarly pitiful number of these unique ‘relics’. Without the ability to detect Psionic energy, isolating any Hashetty tech that may be traveling through independent, or black market channels has proven quite difficult, although we are relatively confident that no single party possesses anything worth special attention.

With that said however, as with everything, the Rats likely have far more than they let on.
