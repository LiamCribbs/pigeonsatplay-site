# The Destruction of Earth and Hardon’s Aquisition

`Emergency Order from Zetricka Juste - Galactic Prime Minister`

Esteemed members of the Galactic Senate, you are to cease all investigations into the Saxon corporation (with regards to the destruction of Earth), effective immediately.

It is to be the official stance of the Federation that they are completely innocent in this matter. My office has been working closely with Saxon representatives over the past few weeks to conduct a large-scale internal investigation of the company, as well as utilizing their resources to sweep through the nearby systems. These efforts have revealed that the Hardon corporation bears full culpability for the attack, and will be prosecuted accordingly. As it stands, there is overwhelming evidence that they abused their security contracts within Luna’s dockyards to gain access to a Gravity-Drive enabled ship and subsequently activated it before it could reach the Minimum Safe Distance.

As for the efforts of your individual teams thus far: all evidence gathered has been determined to largely be Hardon propaganda intended to cripple Saxon’s expansion into arms production and will be labeled as entirely fictitious going forward.

Following this, I am ordering a galactic Search and Seizure of all Hardon assets in cooperation with Saxon military forces (where applicable). Saxon will be effectively absorbing the Hardon corporation throughout this process, with a long-term aim to restructure their assets such that they can continue to operate critical supply-chains, as well as maintain all Hardon equipment currently in use by us (the Federation) and private citizens alike. However, in accordance with the Centralized WMD treaty, you are to *immediately* seize any assets that fit the following criteria:

- Atomic Weapons whose yield is >10kT
- Any electromagnetic ordnance
- Starships of ‘Colony’ class or larger
- Plasma-FTL & Slipspace capable craft
- Any Autonomous Infantry dependent on a centralized control structure
- Any Supermaterial samples
    - With the exception of Saxonite, of course

I thank you for your cooperation in this matter.

*Secure and maintain peace for all life within the galaxy, now and forever.*
