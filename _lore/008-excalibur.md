# Excalibur

`Interview with Private Krat Garner over concerns of misconduct by battalion members.`

**K. Garner:** So, let's just get this outta the way… If you tell anyone about this, I’ll blow those teeth straight out of your mouth. I’m not gonna get shitcanned for reporting to you *exactly* what I saw.

**INTERVIEWER:** I do believe that it's pertinent to remind you that you’re protected from persecution by the parties you're accusing.

**K. Garner:** All thirty six of them, straight out of the gums. I don’t- …We got that straight?

**INTERVIEWER:** Humans have thirty two teeth.

**K. Garner:** Good. Now I got you thinking about it… But I’ll get right to the meat: the surviving members in my battalion are all telling you a complete *lie*. All because they don't want to get flayed for losing our entire stronghold, all of our equipment, hell- probably the entire region by this point, to less than a hundred enemy *footsoldiers*… And one particular sword.

**INTERVIEWER:** The Psion are strong but they’re not invulnerable, Private. Even if each of them were a Psion that still wouldn’t-

**K. Garner:** None of them were. NONE.

**INTERVIEWER:** So I’m assuming you’re going to claim that the blackout your battalion suffered was triggered by some kind of weapon then?

**K. Garner:** Sure was, *ma’am*. A Saxon 680 EMP, fired off in the middle of the night, everyone in their tents, no suspects… I wrote it off as a malfunction, but then I knew something was up when I found all the sim cards taken out of our suits… I think Leiland did that by the way. I think he’s too much of a pussy to have started stiff-arming the *whole* narrative, but he’s definitely part of it somehow.

**INTERVIEWER:** Any proof to speak of private? Or did that go *poof* as well?

**K. Garner:** Listen ma’am, respectfully, all thirty two. No, I’ve got a good chunk of video. Not a whole lot, and it’s shit quality cuz it’s pulled straight from my targeting computer, but it’s 480p and I think it's enough to run with.

**INTERVIEWER:** Alright then. Have you brought it with you?

**K. Garner:** Yes ma’am, but I need you to understand something first.

**INTERVIEWER:** Yes, all thirty two, sure.

**K. Garner:** Not that, but good. No, what you’re going to see, unintelligible as it is, is what looks like the worst days of our fucking lives. It looks like we all stumbled the fuck out of bed, sloshed off our fucking dicks, and pissed away our most valuable possible asset. You’re gonna think that no one in my entire battalion should've ever been let near *any* kind of weapon. But it’s all because… Well I can’t think of it as anything but 'excalibur’...

**INTERVIEWER:** Got it, a Psionic weapon then*.*

**K. Garner:** Yes! But- Have you ever looked at a firing line? Ma’am? No- that's not another threat. But it’s a feeling, a horrible feeling that no matter what you do, you could die instantly right where you stand. I hadn’t seen one, not really as I found out. I got a bit wigged out being downrange during my first week of basic… But that was *nothin…*

**INTERVIEWER:** And it was a sword? *Just* one?

**K. Garner:** If you saw it, you'd know that you wouldn't need another one. And no it’s not the King Arthur pansy bullshit, it’s just the… [ Straining Noise ] The vibe of the thing I don’t know. It was The Sword, the one in all the dictionaries, the first thing on a search result for ‘sword’. And when he came over the ridge with it, all his fuckers in their glass armor comin up behind him, and even when they started shooting at us I couldn't stop looking at the fucking sword… It felt like seeing a million of them looking right at me. Like the whole valley was teaming with the blue fuckers, all ready to gut me like an animal and let me bleed out in my chair… All because that single grunt looked right at me, holding that *thing* down at his side.

**INTERVIEWER:** But you said he wasn't a Psion? That there were *no* Psion, in fact.

**K. Garner:** Correctomundo commander. Plain and simple. He just had the best weapon. The *Best* weapon. So they danced around it. He led every charge, was the front man on every insane maneuver they pulled on us. B-b-because, the Best weapon, that’s- We had howitzers commander, so I know this sounds stupid, but it was the Best weapon on the field. You knew it just by laying eyes on it. I saw bullets spark off it, I saw it stab straight through a tiny, itty bitty hole in my buddy’s canopy- right into his lung! It wasn't some lightsaber thing, it didn't blast through walls or anything like those fucking cannons they got, but it always did everything perfectly… Glanced at the right angle every time, sliced through what it had to, always hit its target one way or another… Be-b-cause how is a sword better than a howitzer commander? It appears exactly where it's needed when it's needed, and the hundred other bastards just had to follow its lead…

**INTERVIEWER:** And again, this is just the one sword?

**K. Garner:** Yeah it was freaky, they normally have a couple you know, you’ve seen em, but this was the only one in the whole gaggle of the fuckers.

**INTERVIEWER:** And he actually fought with it? Against mechs?

**K. Garner:** Against *me* commander. He put the fear of whatever fuckin god they serve right into my spine. And hell, I’ll say it, I wasn’t the *only* one hosing diarrhea outta my cockpit. But he jumped right at me, no fear, no hesitation, just his little toothpick against 50 BMG. Now by this point we’re talkin about this thing on the comms, ‘get that asshole with the sword’, ‘that sword’s gotta go’, ‘anybody got peepers on King Arthur?’ then boom. He’s right there. Now I got a good hundred meters on him so I take my time, let my computer get a nice sight on him, then BAM! I let it rip and some total other guy turns into mist, I didn’t even see him and somehow I blasted him to hell, and sword guy? He’s suddenly off to the left! I know some magic shit’s happening but I got a full mag so I start it emptyin it at him. And now when I tell you he sat down in the grass and prayed? Once my little red counter hit zero, and he was still sitting there, I started fucking *RUNNING*.

**INTERVIEWER:** Running, private?

**K. Garner:** YOU BETCHA COMMANDER! He had a divine mandate to kick our asses. And the best part? He caught up to me, so I fire up my boosters because I wanna get OUT. But right before he’s turned to a crisp the asshole *trips* on the damn thing right before my jets go off. I launch into the air and I just see the little thing shining there in the dirt... So clean and… I- I wanna see it again…

**INTERVIEWER:** Ah… Well I’m certainly excited. May I see these videos now?

**K. Garner:** Oh. Yeah, here’s the thing… Damn I–
